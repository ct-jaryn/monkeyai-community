# Community conduct

Be welcoming, respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and changes rather than people; respect different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, sharing private information without consent and spam are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content, decline contributions or restrict participation to protect the community.

For sensitive conduct concerns, use GitHub's reporting tools: https://support.github.com/contact/abuse . Do not publish personal details in an issue. Maintainers will review concerns as resources permit.
